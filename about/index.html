About me

Hi, I'm Kamaya. I'm eighteen, from Dublin, and I study physics at DCU. I like the problems where hardware and software meet: robots, embedded systems, control loops, and the moment a board I designed lights up for the first time.

I learn by building. At sixteen I designed the lights that let a hospital robot tell people where it's going. Since then I've built a wireless debugger for robots, an AI that reads dental X-rays, an intraoral scanner, and a chainsaw robot armoured in baking tins. At fourteen I sent a camera to the International Space Station, and this year I co-founded Feena and took it to San Francisco.

Away from the bench I swim in the sea, play badminton, listen to a lot of music, and read classic novels.
